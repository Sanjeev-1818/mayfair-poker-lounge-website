The Club

Gurugram's private poker club.

Mayfair Poker Lounge Gurugram is a private members' poker club on the lower level of Limewood Stay Oasis, Sector 52, a few minutes from Golf Course Road and Ardee Mall. Professional dealers, full-size tables and a calm, comfortable room, close to the heart of the city.
