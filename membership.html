Membership

Join Mayfair

A members' club, open to players aged 18 and over.

How to join

Your seat, in three steps.

  1. Enquire

    Message or call the club you would like to visit.

  2. Register

    On your first visit, bring a valid government photo ID. Registration takes a few minutes.

  3. Take your seat

    Join a table whenever the club is open. Reserve ahead on busy evenings.

House Rules

A few simple courtesies.

  • Members and registered guests only, aged 18 and over.
  • Smart casual dress. No sportswear or flip-flops.
  • Phones away from the table while you are in a hand.
  • One player to a hand. No advice from the rail.
  • The dealer's decision is final; the floor host settles disputes.
  • Respect your fellow players and our team at all times.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Do I need to be a member to play?

Yes. Mayfair is a members' club. Membership is quick: bring a valid photo ID on your first visit and we will register you.

Can I bring a guest?

Yes, guests are welcome with a member. They need to be 18 or over and register with a valid photo ID at the door.

Do I need to reserve a seat?

It is not required, but we recommend it on weekends and busy evenings. Message the club on WhatsApp and we will hold your seat.

What should I wear?

Smart casual. Think evening out, not gym or beach.

Can I book a private table?

Yes. Private tables with a dealer can be arranged for groups. Contact the club for availability.

Your seat is waiting.

Message the club you would like to visit, and our team will take care of the rest.
