The Experience

Inside Mayfair

A room designed around one thing: the game.

The Room

Designed for focus.

Our rooms are quiet, comfortable and private. Lighting is set for the game: soft around the room and clear over every table, so cards and chips are always easy to read.

It is calm by design. No noise from a casino floor, no distractions. Just the table, good company and a well-run game.

The Tables

Made to our own specification.

Every Mayfair table is full-size, with nine seats, a dealer position, a cup holder at every seat and a padded rail for comfort.

The centre of the table is kept clear, so nothing competes with the cards.

The People

Looked after, from the door to the table.

Professional dealers

Trained dealers run every table with speed, accuracy and fairness.

A host on the floor

Our team manages seating and waiting lists, so you are never left wondering.

Refreshments

Drinks and light refreshments served at your seat.
