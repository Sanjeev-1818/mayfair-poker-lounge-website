Responsible Play

Play Well

Poker is best enjoyed with a clear head and a set limit.

At Mayfair, we want every member to enjoy the game for the long run. That means playing within your means, taking breaks and knowing when to stop.

  • Entry is strictly for members aged 18 and over, with valid photo ID.
  • Decide your limit before you sit down, and stick to it.
  • Never play with money you need for other things.
  • Take regular breaks. The table will still be there.
  • If you would like a break from the club, ask any member of our team. We will respect your request, privately.

If poker is no longer fun, talk to someone you trust, or speak to a counsellor. Our team is always happy to help you take a step back.
